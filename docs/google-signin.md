# Sign in with Google

This guide explains how to use the Google Sign-In feature.

## Prerequisites

- A valid Google account
- A supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)

## Steps to Sign In

1. Go to the login page
2. Click the **Sign In** button
3. Select **Continue with Google**
4. Choose your Google account from the list
5. You will be redirected to the dashboard

## Troubleshooting

If login fails, check the following:

- Make sure pop-ups are not blocked in your browser
- Clear your browser cache and try again
- Make sure your Google account is active

## How to Sign Out

1. Click your **profile picture** on the top right corner
2. Select **Sign Out** from the dropdown menu
3. You will be returned to the login page

## Notes

- Your session will automatically expire after 30 minutes of inactivity
- Always sign out when using a shared or public computer
